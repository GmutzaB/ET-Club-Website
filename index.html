01 — About

Who we are

Add a line here about how long the club has existed or what makes it distinct.

We're a student-run club for anyone building toward a career in mechatronics, controls, or engineering technology.

Some of us are headed into industrial controls, some into robotics, some still figuring it out — the club exists to give everyone real exposure: facility tours, people working in the field, and enough hands-on time to actually build something before graduation.

02 — What We Do

Four things, done well

Not a new event every week — a small set of things worth showing up for.

▸ Facility Tours

Visits to manufacturers, plants, and shops so members see the equipment and processes their coursework describes, not just the theory.

▸ Build Nights

Hands-on sessions — soldering, basic circuits, small embedded projects members can start and finish in one sitting.

▸ Study Groups

Open sessions for the FE exam and tough coursework — bring a problem set, work through it with people in the same classes.

▸ Project Showcases

Members present whatever they're building — capstones, personal projects, internship work — for practice and feedback.
